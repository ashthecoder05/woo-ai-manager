Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

HeySarva ("we", "us", "our") operates the HeySarva WordPress plugin and the backend service at heysarva.com. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What We Collect

When you use the HeySarva plugin, the following data is sent to our servers:

  • Google account info — your name and email address (used for sign-in and account identification)
  • Store snapshot data — revenue totals, recent order summaries (customer first/last name, order total, status), top-selling products, and low-stock items
  • Your chat messages — the questions you ask the AI
  • WooCommerce API keys — only if you opt in to Advanced mode (stored encrypted)

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Customer email addresses
  • Customer physical addresses or phone numbers
  • Payment or credit card details
  • Passwords
  • Full order line-item details (unless queried in Advanced mode, and not stored)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To answer your questions — store data and your message are sent to Azure OpenAI to generate a response. This data is not stored after the response is returned.
  • To manage your account — your email and credit balance are stored in our database.
  • To improve the service — we track anonymised usage counts (number of chats per day). We do not store the content of your messages or AI responses.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your session token is encrypted with AES-256-CBC before being stored in WordPress
  • WooCommerce API keys (Advanced mode) are stored encrypted in our PostgreSQL database
  • All communication between the plugin and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS
  • Our servers are protected by Cloudflare and UFW firewall
  • Database access is restricted to internal Docker networks only

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In — used for authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Azure OpenAI — used to generate AI responses. Your data is processed under Microsoft's data privacy terms. Azure OpenAI does not use your data to train models.
  • Cloudflare — used for CDN, SSL, and DDoS protection. Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data (email, name, credits) — retained while your account is active
  • Chat content — not stored. Processed in-memory and discarded after each response.
  • WC API keys — retained until you disconnect your store or sign out
  • Usage stats — anonymised chat counts retained indefinitely for analytics

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Sign out from the plugin settings to delete your session and stored WC credentials
  • Request data deletion — email hello@heysarva.com and we will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days
  • Export your data — email us to request a copy of all data we hold about you

8. Cookies

The HeySarva plugin does not set any cookies. The landing page (heysarva.com) does not use cookies or tracking scripts. Cloudflare may set a technical cookie (__cf_bm) for bot protection.

9. Children's Privacy

HeySarva is a business tool for WooCommerce store owners. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the plugin after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email us at hello@heysarva.com.
